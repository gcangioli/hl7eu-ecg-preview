Logical Model: ECG biomarker definition model

Official URL: http://hl7.eu/fhir/ecg/StructureDefinition/EHDSECGBiomarkerDefinition Version: 0.1.0-ci-build
Draft as of 2026-11-06 Computable Name: EHDSECGBiomarkerDefinition

Copyright/Legal: Copyright HL7 Europe. Licensed under creative commons public domain (CC0 1.0).

Registry entry for a derived or AI biomarker, equivalent to FHIR R5 ObservationDefinition. Describes what the biomarker is; the algorithms that compute it and their performance are described in EHDSECGAlgorithm.

Usages:

  • This Logical Model is not used by any profiles in this Specification
