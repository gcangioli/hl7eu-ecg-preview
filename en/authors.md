# Authors and Contributors - HL7 Europe Electrocardiogram (ECG) Report v0.1.0-ci-build

## Authors and Contributors

This Implementation Guide is produced by HL7 Europe, specifically the Project Team for the ECG Report.

**Major contributors and their roles:**

* **HL7 Europe Affiliate members**, who contributed their local expertise and helped ensure alignment with national standards and priorities.
* **HL7 Europe**, which provides a project-agnostic platform for collaborative work, supporting both the technical and administrative development of HL7 Europe's implementation guides. This guide also builds on HL7 Europe's Base and Core FHIR IG, developed by a similar cross-project team.
