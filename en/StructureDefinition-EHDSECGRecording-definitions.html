0. EHDSECGRecording
Definition

The waveform and its acquisition metadata, which the Xt-EHR models do not provide. The metadata are always structured; the samples are carried inline (FHIR SampledData, required for short recordings) or by reference to a native object (long recordings). The time of sample i of a channel, from acquisitionStart, is startOffset + skew + i / samplingFrequency.
