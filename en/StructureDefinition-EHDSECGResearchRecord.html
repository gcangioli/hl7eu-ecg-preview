Logical Model: ECG research record model

Official URL: http://hl7.eu/fhir/ecg/StructureDefinition/EHDSECGResearchRecord Version: 0.1.0-ci-build
Draft as of 2026-11-06 Computable Name: EHDSECGResearchRecord

Copyright/Legal: Copyright HL7 Europe. Licensed under creative commons public domain (CC0 1.0).

Research extraction profile for EHDS secondary use (chapter IV of the Regulation): one record per pseudonymised subject. Shares the semantic core of the care profile, does not require a PDF rendering, and documents what a training or evaluation set needs.

Usages:

  • This Logical Model is not used by any profiles in this Specification
