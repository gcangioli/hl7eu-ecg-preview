Logical Model: ECG result document model

Official URL: http://hl7.eu/fhir/ecg/StructureDefinition/EHDSECGResultDocument Version: 0.1.0-ci-build
Draft as of 2026-11-06 Computable Name: EHDSECGResultDocument

Copyright/Legal: Copyright HL7 Europe. Licensed under creative commons public domain (CC0 1.0).

Transports annotations and derived results produced after the ECG was first exchanged (for example an AI analysis of an archived ECG or a later re-reading), without rebuilding the original report. The objects it carries link to the examination and recordings by their durable identifiers (external links).

Usages:

  • This Logical Model is not used by any profiles in this Specification
